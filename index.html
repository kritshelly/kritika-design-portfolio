Kritika — Designer & curious human

Equal parts big-picture thinker and detail person.

I’m a designer working at the intersection of strategy and digital craft. I like asking the questions that open up new possibilities, then getting hands-on to bring them to life. From making sense of a messy challenge to making that last interaction feel just right.

01 / UNDERSTAND ↗Research & strategyFind a clear direction +

Making the complex clear. User research, opportunity mapping, service design, and a shared direction that turns insights into meaningful next steps.

02 / DESIGN ↗Digital experiencesMake it useful and intuitive +

Bringing ideas into focus. UX architecture, expressive interfaces, interactive prototypes, and design systems that feel as good as they function.

03 / EXPLORE ↗Ideas into prototypesMake possibilities tangible +

Making the future tangible. Co-creation workshops, concept development, rapid experiments, and prototypes that help teams learn before they build.
